ABOUT ME

A Little About Me

Hi! I'm the person behind My Little Sketchbook.

I enjoy turning simple ideas into pencil sketches. For me, sketching is more than just drawing — it's a quiet way to express thoughts, feelings and imagination.

Some of my sketches are inspired by nature, some by people and moments around me, and some simply come from an idea that I wanted to put on paper.
